# 📊 Company Sales Analysis (Google Sheets)
About the project

This project analyzes a global company's sales using Google Sheets. The dataset contains several years of sales data, product categories, and country information. The objective was to clean the data, calculate business metrics, explore sales performance from different perspectives, build visualizations, and perform an ABC analysis based on the Pareto principle.

# Dataset

The project uses three tables:
- Events – sales transactions
- Products – product categories
- Countries – countries, regions, and subregions

## Tools:
- Google Sheets;
- Pivot Tables;
- Charts & Dashboard.

## Project tasks:
- Data quality assessment and missing value handling;
- Revenue, Cost, and Profit calculations;
- KPI analysis;
- Category performance analysis;
- Geographic sales analysis (countries, regions, subregions);
- Online vs Offline sales comparison;
- Shipping time analysis;
- Correlation analysis between shipping time and profit;
- Sales trends by year, month, and weekday;
- ABC analysis (Pareto principle);
- Dashboard creation.

## Key Insights:
🌍 The company operates in 46 countries.
💰 Overall profit margin equals 29.46%.
🏆 Office Supplies, Household, and Cosmetics generate the highest revenue and profit.
🇪🇺 Around 89.45% of total profit comes from the European market, with Southern Europe being the strongest-performing subregion.
🛒 Online and offline sales perform almost equally, while online sales exceed offline by approximately 1%.
🚚 Shipping time shows no meaningful correlation with profit, suggesting profitability is driven mainly by product category, pricing, and sales volume.
📈 Sales fluctuate over time, with the highest profit recorded in 2014.
📅 Friday is the strongest sales day, while Thursday has the lowest sales volume.
📊 ABC analysis confirms the Pareto principle: a small number of product categories generates most of the company's revenue and profit.

## Business Recommendations
1. Focus inventory and marketing investments on Office Supplies, Household, and Cosmetics.
2. Continue expanding in high-performing European markets.
3. Optimize pricing and product mix rather than prioritizing shipping speed.
4. Use ABC analysis to improve inventory management and resource allocation.
